ZombieHold-Out

The dead are coming in waves. Shoot them down, earn coins, and build barricades and turrets between rounds. How many waves can you survive?

Keyboard + mouse: WASD move, aim with the mouse, hold click to shoot, R reload
Gamepad: left stick moves, right stick aims, RT shoots, X reloads
Phone: left thumb moves, right thumb aims and fires
Build phase: pick an item in the shop bar and click or tap the ground to place it. Gamepad: LB/RB pick, A places in front of you, Y starts the wave.
