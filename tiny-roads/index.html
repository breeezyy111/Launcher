Left click or drag: build. Right click or drag: erase (or use the Erase tool on touch). Roads only connect where you draw them, so drag from a house or station along the road to link it. Roads over water use a bridge. Motorway tool: drag from one road tile to another in a straight line (any distance) to build an elevated motorway that passes over everything in between; right click an end to remove it. Light tool: left click a road to place a light, right click to remove it.
