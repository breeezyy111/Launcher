Block BashArena

Ram your rivals off the edge. The stage shrinks every second. Last bumper standing takes the round, first to 3 rounds wins.

Player 1: WASD move, Space bash
Player 2: arrow keys move, Enter or / bash
Gamepads: stick to move, A or RB to bash, Start to pause
Phone: drag anywhere to steer, tap BASH. For 2 players, lay the phone flat: each player gets their own half.
Pickups: ⬢ Mega makes you huge and heavy, ⚡ Zip makes you fast.
