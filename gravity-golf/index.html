GravityGolf

Nine holes across the stars. Planets pull your ball, so curve your shots around them and land in the cup. Watch out for black holes.

Mouse or touch: drag back from anywhere and let go, like a slingshot
Keyboard: ← → aim, ↑ ↓ power, Space shoot, R retry from last spot (+1)
Gamepad: stick aims, d-pad ↑ ↓ or triggers set power, A shoots
Lost in space? The ball comes back after a few seconds with a one-stroke penalty.
Birdie!
