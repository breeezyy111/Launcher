GravityGolf

Mini golf across the stars. Planets pull your ball, so curve your shots around them and land in the cup. 36 holes over four courses, from gentle orbits to black holes.

Mouse or touch: drag back from anywhere and let go, like a slingshot. The dots show where the shot starts to go.
Keyboard: ← → aim (Shift for fine), ↑ ↓ power, Space shoot, R reset ball (+1)
Gamepad: stick aims, triggers or d-pad ↑ ↓ set power, A shoots, Y resets
Stars: one for sinking it, one for par or better, one for grabbing every stardust ✦ on the hole.

Pick a course

Play all nine holes for a score, or tap a single hole to practise it. Finish a course to open the next one.
