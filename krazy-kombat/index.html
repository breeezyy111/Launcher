How to play

Rules

Knock your rival's health to zero to win the round. First to two rounds wins the match. Land hits to fill your Krazy meter. When it glows, hold down + Special for a Krazy Rush super. Win the match and you get a few seconds of Krazy Time: press Special for your fighter's finisher.

Moves (every fighter)

Punch, Kick, Down+Punch = uppercut, Down+Kick = sweep (hits low), Toward+Punch up close = throw, jump with Punch or Kick in the air. Block with the Block button, or hold away from your rival when they attack. Crouch-block low sweeps. Jump attacks must be blocked standing.

Specials

Special = projectile or signature move. Toward+Special and Up+Special do the other two. Each fighter's list is below.

Player 1 keys

Move WASD · Punch F · Kick G · Special H · Block R. Against the CPU you can also use the arrow keys with K L ; O.

Player 2 keys

Move with the arrow keys · Punch K or Num 1 · Kick L or Num 2 · Special ; or Num 3 · Block O or Num 0

Controllers and touch

Gamepads work: stick or d-pad to move, X punch, A kick, B special, bumpers block. First pad is Player 1, second is Player 2. On a phone, use the on-screen stick and buttons. Pause with Esc or P.
